<objective>
Implement the core of the redesign: the entry-point dispatch and the `_traverse_hotspot_with_children` method that enforces the "waypoint + one level of real child chunks, decision on children" contract. This is the change that short-circuits the `node_stats=None` early-return which is the proximate cause of Q18's empty hits.

Purpose: When the `HybridClusterHotspotSelector` returns a route-like parent as the hotspot, the parent has no direct vectors and is filtered from `node_stats_list`. The original `_traverse_from_node` then returns `[]`, triggering the chunk_id-less fallback. This plan makes hotspot traversal collect direct children directly from `node_by_id` (independent of `node_stats` for the parent), emit the parent as a waypoint, build real evidence hits from evidence-bearing children, and delegate the drill-down decision to `policy.evaluate_children` on child stats.

Output:
- Hotspot-detection block inserted after `start_nodes` resolution (after line 1665) in `traverse_tree_for_query`.
- New method `RecursiveTreeTraversalRunner._traverse_hotspot_with_children(...)`.
- P13-01..P13-06 and P13-10 tests flipped from skip to GREEN.
</objective>
