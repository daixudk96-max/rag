<threat_model>
  <threat id="T-09-01" severity="high" stride="Tampering">
    A milestone commit could accidentally include unrelated modified or untracked files from the dirty working tree.
    Mitigation: produce an explicit classification report with `excluded_from_staging` and no staging commands.
  </threat>
  <threat id="T-09-02" severity="high" stride="Information Disclosure">
    Dirty-tree inspection could expose or serialize raw secrets such as `DATABASE_URL`.
    Mitigation: classify secret-sensitive paths by name/status only; never read or write raw secret values.
  </threat>
  <threat id="T-09-03" severity="medium" stride="Repudiation">
    Future reviewers may not know why files were included or excluded.
    Mitigation: include rationale per bucket in `classification_report.md` and machine-readable `classification_report.json`.
  </threat>
</threat_model>
