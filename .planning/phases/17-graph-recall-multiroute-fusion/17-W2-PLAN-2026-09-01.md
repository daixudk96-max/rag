2. map_entity_mentions(uie_entities, *, document_id, version_id, normalized_text) -> UieMappingResult{candidates, rejected}

4. map_relation_triples(uie_relations, schema, *, document_id, version_id, normalized_text) -> {triples, rejected}
   - RelationTriple frozen: subject_text/subject_type/relation/object_text/object_type/source_text/document_id/version_id — 出处句强制
   - 门控: relation 不在 schema 或 subject_type 不符 → 拒收记录 reason; canonical 类型映射表 UIE 类型 → PER/LOC/CORP/PROD 显式写死, 未知类型拒收
